User & Group Management

Department groups and directory permissions with chown, chmod, SGID and the sticky bit.

Overview

Department groups and user accounts were created, memberships assigned and directory access controlled with chown, chmod, SGID and the sticky bit.

Objectives

  • Restrict users to authorised directories.

What was done

  • Created groups and users and assigned memberships.
  • Set ownership and permissions on department directories.
  • Applied SGID and the sticky bit.

Screenshots

In the order they appear in the repository. Click one to see it full size.

Testing and verification

  • New files inherited group ownership under SGID.
  • Users could not delete others' files in the sticky-bit directory.
